## Experiment Framework

### Three-Phase Model

```

Validate Phase:
- Duration: [1-2 weeks]

- Objective: [What patterns validate ICP?]
- Decision Point: [When to decide to move to Scale]

Scale Phase:
- Duration: [2-4 weeks]
- Purpose: Scale ICP engagement

- Decision Point: [When to decide to continue/optimize]
```
