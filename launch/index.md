# Launch & Iterate

**Purpose**: Launch private beta and experiment your way to success

**Outcome**: Clear ICP finding your platform and staying, enabling continuous learning and validation

**Audience**: PM / Dev / Both

**Time**: 4-8 weeks (private beta) + ongoing iteration

**Prerequisites**: [Build the MVP](../build/index.md) - Working MVP, [Decide What to Build](../decide/index.md) - Initial ICP research

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this section, you will be able to:

1. Develop a comprehensive B2B ICP framework aligned with private beta success
2. Create messaging strategy that resonates with your ICP across all channels
3. Design and execute channel strategies for outbound and growth marketing
4. Build a conversion-optimized website V1 with experimentation focus
5. Set up a complete tool stack for launch operations and tracking
6. Launch private beta with both deployment and GTM execution
7. Run experiments across Outbound, Growth Marketing, and Product using the three-phase model
8. Synthesize insights across all experiments to inform decisions
9. Define exit criteria and transition to public beta

## Worked Example

**Situation**: Launching retrospective tool MVP to private beta

**Launch Process**:

1. **Prepare ICP**: B2B ICP = Engineering teams (5-15 people) at SaaS companies (50-500 employees), targeting Engineering Managers
2. **Develop Messaging**: "Turn retrospectives into action" - focused on pain of wasted time, gain of actionable insights
3. **Design Channel Strategy**: LinkedIn ads targeting Engineering Managers, email outreach to communities, content marketing
4. **Build Website V1**: Landing page with clear value prop, signup CTA, experimentation on messaging variations
5. **Setup Tool Stack**: Webflow website, Mixpanel analytics, Buffer for social, ChatGPT for content
6. **Launch Private Beta**: Deploy MVP, activate channels, send to 20 target ICP prospects
7. **Run Experiments**:
   * Explore: Test LinkedIn ad messaging (2 days)
   * Validate: Test ICP sub-verticals (1 week)
   * Scale: Scale winning channel (2 weeks)
8. **Synthesize Insights**: Engineering Managers at 50-200 employee SaaS companies showing highest engagement
9. **Exit Criteria**: 60% retention, 30% paid conversion → Ready for public beta

**Result**: Clear ICP validated, repeatable acquisition channel identified, product-market fit signals present
