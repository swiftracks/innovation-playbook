### 4. Monitor and Validate ICP

**ICP Validation Monitoring**:

**Signals to Track**:
- **Signup Rate**: Are ICP signing up?
- **Activation Rate**: Are ICP completing first action?
- **Retention Rate**: Are ICP returning?
- **Engagement Rate**: Are ICP using core features?
- **Feedback Quality**: Is feedback from ICP?

**ICP Validation Metrics**:
- **Explore Phase**: Quick signals (days)
  - Are ICP signing up?
  - Are ICP engaging?
  - Is feedback from ICP?
- **Validate Phase**: Strong signals (1-2 weeks)
  - Are ICP staying?
  - Are ICP using repeatedly?
  - Are ICP providing quality feedback?
- **Scale Phase**: Confirmed signals (2-4 weeks)
  - Are ICP converting to paid?
  - Is acquisition repeatable?
  - Is product-market fit emerging?

**If ICP Not Validating**:
- **Wrong ICP**: Explore other ICP segments
- **Wrong Messaging**: Test messaging variations
- **Wrong Channels**: Test other channels
- **Product Issues**: Fix product issues
- **All Above**: Iterate based on learnings

**Document in Swift CNS**:
- Track ICP validation metrics
- Document ICP validation signals
- Create ICP validation experiments
