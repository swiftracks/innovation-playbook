# Program Operations

**Purpose**: Help teams and cohorts run SwiftCNS with repeatable cadence and quality gates.

## Outcome

Consistent execution and clearer decision governance across multiple teams.

This section matters most when SwiftCNS is no longer being used by one team in isolation. As soon as multiple teams, mentors, or program operators are involved, the work needs more than good workflow pages. It needs operating discipline.

## Includes

- [Cadence and Stage Gates](cadence-and-stage-gates.md)
- [Mentor Collaboration Model](mentor-collaboration-model.md)

## When to use

- You are running one or more teams in parallel.
- You need consistent review standards and escalation rules.
- You want portfolio-level visibility into cycle quality and speed.

## Why this layer matters

Strong teams can still struggle in weak operating environments.

Without clear program operations:

- cycles slip without anyone noticing,
- review quality varies wildly between teams,
- mentors get involved too late or too inconsistently,
- decisions are hard to compare across a portfolio,
- and leadership sees activity without understanding learning quality.

This section helps turn individual team effort into a more reliable system.

## What good looks like

At this layer, good looks like:

- teams know when they will be reviewed,
- stage-gate expectations are explicit,
- mentors know where they add the most value,
- program managers can spot weak cycles early,
- and decisions become more comparable across teams.
