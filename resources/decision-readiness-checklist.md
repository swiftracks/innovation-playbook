# Decision Readiness Checklist

Before making a decision, confirm:

- [ ] The highest-risk assumption was tested.
- [ ] Hypothesis and success criteria were defined before execution.
- [ ] Experiment evidence is documented and interpretable.
- [ ] Learnings are validated and confidence-rated.
- [ ] Insights synthesize implications and trade-offs.
- [ ] Decision options are explicit (go, iterate, pivot, stop).
- [ ] Decision owner and next action are assigned.

If any box is unchecked, do not finalize the decision yet.

## Why this checklist matters

Teams often think they are ready to decide when they are really just ready to discuss. This checklist helps separate the two.

The point is not to slow decisions down. It is to make sure a decision is being made on real footing rather than momentum, pressure, or intuition alone.

## How to use this in practice

Use it before a decision meeting, not halfway through one. Review it with the actual decision owner present. If a box is unchecked, name the smallest missing step needed to resolve it. And do not turn the checklist into a reason to delay forever. It is a readiness tool, not an excuse to avoid commitment.

## What good looks like

A decision-ready team can explain what assumption was tested, what learning is driving the decision, what uncertainty remains, why the decision is still reasonable now, and what happens next.
