# 06 — Synthesize Insights → Decision

> **Purpose**: Synthesize all learnings into a clear go/no-go decision
> **Outcome**: Make a data-driven decision about what to build (or pivot)
> **Audience**: PM / Dev / Both
> **Time**: 2-3 hours
> **Prerequisites**: [05 — Extract Key Learnings](05-extract-key-learnings.md)

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
1. Synthesize learnings from multiple experiments
2. Assess overall risk and opportunity
3. Make a clear go/no-go decision
4. Document the decision and rationale
5. Plan next steps based on decision

## Jobs-to-Be-Done

- **When**: I have analyzed experiment results and extracted learnings
- **I want**: To synthesize insights and make a decision
- **So that**: I can proceed with building or pivot to a better direction

## Inputs

- Learnings summary from [05 — Extract Key Learnings](05-extract-key-learnings.md)
- Hypothesis status for all tested hypotheses
- Experiment results and analysis
- Problem statement and value proposition

## Activities

### 1. Synthesize All Learnings

Combine learnings from all experiments:

**Create a Synthesis**:
- What validated?
- What invalidated?
- What's still unknown?
- What patterns emerged?
- What surprised you?

**Example Synthesis**:
```
Validated:
- Technical approach is viable (AI can generate useful insights)
- Market interest exists (23% conversion, below target but present)

Invalidated:
- Value proposition needs refinement (users hesitant about commitment)
- Pricing model unclear (no clear willingness to pay signal)

Unknown:
- Long-term usage patterns
- Competitive position

Patterns:
- Interest exists but commitment is low
- Technical feasibility is high
- Need to clarify ongoing value

Surprises:
- Users liked AI insights more than expected
- Commitment was lower than expected
```

### 2. Assess Overall Risk

Evaluate risk and opportunity:

**Risk Assessment**:
- **High Risk**: Critical assumptions invalidated
- **Medium Risk**: Some assumptions unclear
- **Low Risk**: Most assumptions validated

**Opportunity Assessment**:
- **High Opportunity**: Large market, strong need
- **Medium Opportunity**: Moderate market, clear need
- **Low Opportunity**: Small market, unclear need

**Example**:
```
Risk: Medium
- Technical risk: Low (validated)
- Market risk: Medium (interest but low commitment)
- Business risk: Medium (pricing unclear)

Opportunity: Medium
- Market size: Moderate
- Need: Clear but requires refinement
- Competition: Moderate
```

### 3. Make the Decision

Use a decision framework:

**Decision Criteria**:
- Are critical assumptions validated?
- Is the risk acceptable?
- Is the opportunity sufficient?
- Do we have a clear path forward?

**Decision Options**:
- **Go**: Proceed to build MVP
- **No-Go**: Don't build, pivot or stop
- **Pivot**: Change direction based on learnings
- **More Learning**: Need more experiments

**Example Decision Framework**:
```
Critical Assumptions:
- Technical feasibility: Validated ✓
- User interest: Partially validated (needs refinement)
- Value proposition: Needs refinement

Risk: Medium (acceptable)
Opportunity: Medium (sufficient)

Decision: **Pivot** - Refine value proposition, test again
```

### 4. Document the Decision

Create a decision document:

**Decision Document Structure**:
```
Decision: [Go/No-Go/Pivot/More Learning]
Date: [Date]
Rationale: [Why this decision]
Key Learnings:
  - [Learning 1]
  - [Learning 2]
  - [Learning 3]
Risk Assessment: [Risk level and reasons]
Opportunity Assessment: [Opportunity level and reasons]
Next Steps: [What to do next]
```

### 5. Plan Next Steps

Based on your decision:

**If Go**:
- Proceed to [Build the MVP](build/index.md)
- Use validated learnings to inform build
- Address any remaining risks

**If No-Go**:
- Stop work on this idea
- Document learnings for future reference
- Consider other ideas or problems

**If Pivot**:
- Refine value proposition or approach
- Test new direction
- Iterate based on learnings

**If More Learning**:
- Design additional experiments
- Test remaining assumptions
- Revisit decision after more data
