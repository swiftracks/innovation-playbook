# Checklist: Identify Critical Assumptions

Use this checklist to verify you've completed the chapter requirements.

## Assumptions List
- [ ] All assumptions are listed
- [ ] Assumptions are categorized (User/Technical/Business/Value)
- [ ] Risk level is assessed for each assumption
- [ ] Impact is assessed for each assumption
- [ ] Evidence is documented for each assumption

## Critical Assumptions
- [ ] Critical assumptions are identified
- [ ] Critical assumptions meet criteria (if false, solution fails; high uncertainty; high impact)
- [ ] Top 5-10 critical assumptions are documented

## Prioritization
- [ ] Assumptions are prioritized by risk and impact
- [ ] Risk matrix is created
- [ ] Top priority assumptions are identified

## Documentation
- [ ] Assumptions list is documented
- [ ] Critical assumptions are documented
- [ ] Prioritization is documented
- [ ] Ready to proceed to [03 — Form Testable Hypotheses](03-form-testable-hypotheses.md)

## Ready to Proceed
- [ ] All items above are complete
- [ ] Top 5-10 critical assumptions are identified and prioritized
- [ ] Ready to proceed to [03 — Form Testable Hypotheses](03-form-testable-hypotheses.md)
