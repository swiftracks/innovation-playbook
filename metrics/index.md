# Metrics and Continuous Improvement

**Purpose**: Measure whether your learning system is getting faster and better over time.

## Outcome

Teams and programs can systematically improve learning velocity and decision quality.

Metrics matter because teams are often better at feeling busy than knowing whether the system is improving. This section helps turn cycle performance into something visible, discussable, and improvable.

## Read next

- [North Star and Supporting Metrics](north-star-and-supporting-metrics.md)
- [Cycle Improvement Playbook](cycle-improvement-playbook.md)

## Why this section matters

Without a measurement layer, teams can confuse motion with progress. They may run more experiments, hold more meetings, or produce more artifacts while still learning slowly.

This section helps answer better questions:

- Are cycles getting shorter?
- Are learnings getting stronger?
- Are decisions getting clearer?
- Are teams improving, or just repeating the same weak patterns?

## How to use this section

Start with the north star and supporting metrics if you need to understand what to measure. Move to the improvement playbook when the numbers suggest the system is drifting or when a team is repeatedly getting stuck in the same part of the loop.
