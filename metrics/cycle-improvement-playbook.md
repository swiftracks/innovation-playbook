# Cycle Improvement Playbook

## Purpose

Provide a practical method for improving cycle performance when speed, quality, or confidence starts to degrade.

## Outcome

Teams identify the highest-impact bottleneck, apply a targeted fix, and verify improvement over subsequent cycles.

Use this when cycle time rises or learning quality drops.

Think of this page less like a formal improvement framework and more like a practical way to ask, “Where is the system actually weakening, and what is the smallest fix that would help?”

## Step 1: Diagnose the bottleneck

Look for the real source of drag:

- assumption quality issue,
- experiment design issue,
- learning synthesis issue,
- decision governance issue.

The key here is precision. Do not say “the team is slow” if the real issue is a weak hypothesis or an unclear decision owner. Improvement starts with naming the actual bottleneck.

## Step 2: Apply targeted intervention

Possible interventions include tightening definitions and scope, reducing experiment batch size, increasing mentor review at weak checkpoints, or enforcing stage-gate deadlines and ownership.

Use the smallest intervention that solves the real problem. Large process changes are tempting, but they often create more noise than clarity.

## Step 3: Validate improvement

Track median cycle time over the next 2-3 cycles, track rubric score and rework rate, and confirm that decision confidence improves.

If an intervention makes the system faster but confidence worse, it is not really an improvement.

## Step 4: Standardize

If intervention works, update team operating practices and program templates.

## What good improvement looks like

Good improvement usually feels modest but meaningful. Assumptions get sharper, experiments get more decisive, learnings become easier to trust, and decisions happen with less friction.

That is how the system compounds. Not through one dramatic process change, but through repeated removal of weak links in the cycle.

## Definition of done

- Bottleneck was diagnosed at the correct checkpoint.
- Intervention improved both speed and confidence quality.
- Successful fix is documented and reused.
